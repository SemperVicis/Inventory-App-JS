Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Inventory App handles information while you use this browser-based application.

Local Storage Usage

The app stores inventory data, category data, language preferences, and consent choices in your browser local storage. This information remains on your device unless you clear browser data or reset the application.

Data Handling

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit your inventory entries to an external server. Any deployment platform may still process basic technical information such as IP address and request logs.

Your Choices

You may accept or decline the local storage notice. You can also clear saved data at any time through your browser settings.
